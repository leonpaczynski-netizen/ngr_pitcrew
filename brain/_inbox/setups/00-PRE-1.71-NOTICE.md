# ⚠️ EVERY SETUP SHEET IN THIS FOLDER IS PRE-1.71

**Opened 21 August 2026. Applies to all nine archived sheets.**

**GT7 v1.71 (20 August 2026) changed the physics** — the tyre slipping model, per-car steering geometry, damper attenuation, the default settings **and the adjustment ranges** of suspension, differential and aerodynamics, Performance Points fleet-wide, both driving assists, and the damage model. Polyphony reset every ranking board in the game.

**Full changelog and the re-measurement protocol: `../16-update-1.71-physics-change.md`.**

---

## What these sheets are now

**They are the historical record and the control group. They are not values to type into the game.**

Every sheet below was built and run on **v1.70 or earlier**. The numbers on them were correct for the game they were run in. Three things make them unsafe to reuse directly:

1. **The adjustment ranges moved.** PD state it in the official notes. Any value expressed as percent-of-range, or as clicks above a minimum, **has silently changed meaning** — and `../11-car-slider-ranges.md` documents that exact failure mode happening twice already for other reasons.
2. **The physics under the values moved.** A camber figure, a diff setting or a damper value was chosen as a response to how the car behaved. That behaviour is what 1.71 changed.
3. **The saved tunes may not even be intact.** The notes say nothing about what happens to existing car settings. They may be unchanged, reset to new defaults, or clamped by a moved range. **Nobody has confirmed which** — see Job 0 below.

## Why they are being kept, not deleted

**Three reasons, and the first is the important one.**

**1. The diagnoses are the valuable part, and diagnoses survive patches.** *"Power-on mid-corner understeer from lap 1, resolved by dropping LSD accel from 18 to 14"* is a reasoning chain about restricted builds and diff torque. Only the two numbers are version-dependent. The same is true of *"the corner that stayed silent was the tell"* and of every driver report in these files.

**2. They are the control group.** When a car is re-baselined on 1.71, the pre-1.71 sheet is the only thing to compare it against. Delete it and the comparison is gone — and a comparison is how you find out what the patch actually did to *your* car, which is more useful than anything the community will publish this month.

**3. The 17 August win.** `2026-08-17-huracan-watkins-glen-long-revC.md` is the reference for what a working setup on this car felt like. Rev D exists to build on it.

## The rule

> **A pre-1.71 sheet may be used as a starting point and as a comparison. It may not be used as a setup.**
>
> **And per Standing Rule 9 — confirm which sheet is physically in the car before diagnosing anything.** That rule already exists because the Pit Crew `setup` block was stale three sessions out of three. A physics patch that may have reset or clamped saved values makes it doubly required.

## Before opening any of them: Job 0

**Two minutes, and it changes what you do next.**

Open the **Huracán** and read its *current* settings screen against `2026-08-17-huracan-watkins-glen-long-revD.md` — **before changing anything.**

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Values unchanged** | Saved tunes survived the patch | Proceed to the range re-read (`16` §12 Job 1) |
| **Values reset to new defaults** | The garage is gone | Re-enter each car from its most recent archived sheet **first** — these files become operationally essential, not historical |
| **Some changed, others not** | The changed ones were **clamped by a moved range** | **Most informative outcome.** The clamped values map out where the new endpoints are before you touch a slider. Record every one. |

**Write down which one happened, verbatim.** It is the first post-1.71 measurement this programme will take, it costs two minutes, and it determines whether the next hour is a re-read or a rebuild.

## What replaces these sheets

Nothing yet — deliberately. **No post-1.71 setup exists for any of these cars, and none should be invented.** The route to one is `../16-update-1.71-physics-change.md` §12, in order:

- **Job 0** — did the tunes survive? *(2 min)*
- **Job 1** — re-read the slider ranges, all three cars, plus step sizes and the LSD floor. **Blocks every sheet.** *(15 min)*
- **Job 2** — one measured stint: Huracán, Watkins Glen Long, Racing Soft, league multiplier, full fuel, to *felt* fall-off. Take the **rear-left gauge reading at lap 6 and lap 12**. *(30 min)*

**Forty-seven minutes rebuilds the foundation.** Then a sheet can be issued — and per Standing Rule 10, it carries its game version.
