# v1.71 — Measured Results

**The running ledger of what has actually been measured on GT7 v1.71.** Companion to `16-update-1.71-physics-change.md`, which says what changed and what to test. This file says what we *found*.

**Rule for this document: nothing enters it that was not measured on v1.71, and every entry carries its sample size and its confounds.** Where a number is provisional it says so. Where a comparison is not like-for-like it says so.

**Source for everything below:** Pit Crew database, `data/pitcrew.db`, session 60 (21 Aug 2026, 17:14–17:38), 12 laps, plus 24 pre-patch sessions on the same car and circuit for the baseline distribution. Telemetry decoded from the stored 60 Hz frame blobs.

# 1. ✅ THE HEADLINE — driven-wheel slip under power fell by 40–60 %

**This is 1.71's slipping-regime rework, measured, and it is the largest confirmed change on this car.**

Rear-wheel slip ratio at full throttle (wheel surface speed ÷ road speed; 1.000 = rolling true, >1 = spinning up). Median across all qualifying frames, **pre-patch = sessions 52, 53, 58 pooled** (three 26-lap races, same car, same circuit, same compound):

| Gear | Pre-patch | 21 Aug short-shift | 21 Aug **full RPM** | Change | Reduction in slip |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1.1004 | 1.0463 | **1.0479** | −5.25 pts | **−52 %** |
| 2 | 1.0635 | 1.0256 | **1.0256** | −3.79 pts | **−60 %** |
| 3 | 1.0494 | 1.0206 | **1.0201** | −2.93 pts | **−59 %** |
| 4 | 1.0385 | 1.0157 | **1.0148** | −2.37 pts | **−62 %** |
| 5 | 1.0322 | 1.0131 | **1.0122** | −2.00 pts | **−62 %** |
| 6 | 1.0278 | 1.0115 | **1.0109** | −1.69 pts | **−61 %** |

**Percentage of full-throttle frames above the 1.02 wheelspin threshold:**

| Gear | s52 | s53 | s58 | **s60 (v1.71)** |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 96.7 % | 95.7 % | 94.9 % | 96.9 % |
| 2 | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % | 97.9 % |
| 3 | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % | **56.6 %** |
| 4 | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % | **6.8 %** |
| 5 | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % | **1.1 %** |
| 6 | 99.7 % | 99.7 % | 99.7 % | **0.6 %** |

### Why this is [MEASURED] and not [INFERRED]

Four confounds were checked and all four are closed:

1. **Not a driving artefact.** Short-shifted laps and full-RPM laps give **the same slip figures** (1.0463 vs 1.0479 in 1st; 1.0256 vs 1.0256 in 2nd). Short-shifting reduces torque at the wheel and should suppress slip — it did not move the number. **Whatever changed is not in the driver's right foot.**
2. **Not a gearing change.** Gear ratios are **byte-identical** pre and post: `2.727 / 1.925 / 1.529 / 1.288 / 1.152 / 1.062`. Job 0 is confirmed independently by the telemetry, not just by reading the screen.
3. **Not a tyre-radius change.** `tyre_radius_m` reads **0.355 m** in every session, before and after.
4. **Not a compound difference.** Racing Hard throughout — sessions 52, 53, 58 and 60.

### The corroborating signal

Engine rpm per km/h at full throttle fell in **every** gear, and the drop scales with gear exactly as the slip drop does — because inflated wheel speed is what was inflating engine rpm:

| Gear | rpm/kph pre | rpm/kph 21 Aug | Δ | (slip Δ, for comparison) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 80.49 | 76.04 | −5.52 % | −5.81 pts |
| 2 | 54.44 | 52.49 | −3.57 % | −3.77 pts |
| 3 | 42.62 | 41.44 | −2.77 % | −2.89 pts |
| 4 | 35.50 | 34.72 | −2.24 % | −2.31 pts |
| 5 | 31.55 | 30.96 | −1.90 % | −1.95 pts |
| 6 | 28.96 | 28.50 | −1.59 % | −1.64 pts |

**The two columns agree to within a fifth of a point at every gear.** They are the same physical change seen through two independent channels. **The pre-patch rpm/kph spread across 24 sessions was 28.86–28.96 in 6th — a total range of 0.35 %. The new value, 28.4997, is 1.4 % below the bottom of it.** That is far outside anything the baseline ever did.

### ⚠️ What this does NOT establish

**It does not say the tyre has more peak grip.** It says the *slipping regime* — how the tyre behaves once it is sliding — is different, which is what the patch notes claim. Steady-state longitudinal slip under power is much lower. **Cornering grip, braking grip and the limit of adhesion are separate questions and none of them were measured here.**

# 2. ✅ Top speed is down, and it is outside the whole pre-patch range

**Vmax in 6th, sustained full throttle (≥15 consecutive frames >95 % throttle), median of the fastest 20 frames:**

| | Value |
|---|---|
| **21 Aug (v1.71)** | **274.9 km/h @ 7,826 rpm** |
| Pre-patch median, 20 practice sessions | 278.75 km/h |
| Pre-patch range, 20 practice sessions | **275.6 – 282.1 km/h** |
| Delta vs median | **−3.9 km/h (−1.4 %)** |

**274.9 sits below the minimum of twenty pre-patch practice sessions.** This is precisely the test `16` §12 specified — *does the delta fall outside the historical spread* — and it does, by 0.7 km/h below the floor.

**Races were excluded from the baseline deliberately**, because a tow inflates Vmax and session 60 was a lobby practice. Including the three races does not change the conclusion.

> **⚠️ Note the sign, because it is counter-intuitive alongside §1.** Less wheelspin usually means *more* usable thrust. Top speed nonetheless fell. The two are consistent if **resistance rose** — which is the other thing 1.71 says it changed *("road surface resistance (rolling resistance) has been optimised")*. **This run cannot separate rolling resistance from aerodynamic drag.** Both act on terminal speed and neither is directly observable here.

# 3. ⚠️ Fuel — provisional, and the two run modes disagree in direction

**Like-for-like comparison (map 1, full RPM, clean practice):**

| | L/lap |
|---|---|
| 11 Aug documented baseline (`Rev B`) | 6.566 (confirmed 6.571) |
| **21 Aug, laps 11–12** | **6.663 and 6.808 → mean 6.736** |
| Delta | **+2.6 %** |

Pre-patch full-RPM practice sessions ran **6.198 – 6.662 L/lap** across five sessions. **6.736 is above all of them** — but on **n = 2 laps**, which is not enough, and it is directionally consistent with §2.

**The complication:** the short-shifted laps went the *other* way. Post-patch short-shift median is **5.282 L/lap** against pre-patch race medians of **5.433 – 5.585**. Those races carried tows and unrecorded fuel-map changes, so they are not a clean control — **which is exactly why this row is ⚠️ and not ✅.**

**Status: [PROVISIONAL]. Two full-RPM laps is not a fuel measurement.** Settle it with five clean full-RPM laps at map 1 in a practice lobby.

# 5. ✅ The rev limiter did NOT move

`16` §12 flagged this as the tripwire: *"the RSR is not on 1.71's max-RPM list — if it reads differently, something bigger happened."* **Nothing bigger happened.**

| Session | Limiter-flagged frames | Median rpm | Peak rpm |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 53 (pre-patch race) | 60 | 8,686 | 8,843 |
| 58 (pre-patch race) | 79 | 8,704 | 8,933 |
| **60 (v1.71)** | **90** | **8,596** | **9,057** |

**The limiter fires in the same place, ~8,600 rpm.** The 9,057 peak is a first-gear transient; pre-patch sessions show the same overshoot at 8,843–8,933. **The documented 8,600 rpm stands, unchanged, on v1.71.**

> **A correction worth recording, because it is the failure mode this whole exercise exists to prevent.** The first read of this data took the 9,057 peak as a raised limiter and briefly treated it as a major finding. **Running the identical query over the pre-patch sessions killed it in one step.** A post-patch number is not a finding until the same measurement has been taken on the same channel pre-patch. **That is now Standing Rule 13.**

### ⭐ The 0/0/0 report is CONFIRMED

`16` §5 carried it as **[COMMUNITY — single source]**, one player, two days after release. **It is now [MEASURED — IN HOUSE].** All three LSD parameters floor at 0.

### And the part nobody reported: the three LSD axes no longer share a scale

On v1.70 all three ran **5–60**. They now run **0–30**, **0–100** and **0–99** respectively. **This is the percent-of-range trap that `11` documents twice, arriving for a third time and worse than before** — a value of 30 used to mean the same proportion on all three axes and now means 100 %, 30 % and 30.3 % of three different ranges.

**Consequence for the sheet, and it is immediate:** the RSR's Rev B diff reads **initial 5 / accel 14 / braking 24** on a 5–60 scale. Those same absolute numbers now sit at **17 % / 14 % / 24 %** of their new ranges, against **0 % / 16 % / 35 %** before. **The absolute values are unchanged and the car drives as built — but every proportional statement about them has silently changed meaning.** Express LSD in absolutes only until the whole register is re-read.

> ⚠️ **Caveat on the comparison.** The v1.70 column is the *Huracán's* register, because the RSR's own pre-patch register was overwritten by the 21 Aug re-read. Both are Gr.3 and both recorded identical endpoints on all five moved parameters, so the change is almost certainly the version. **Re-read the Huracán on v1.71 to make it airtight** — 5 minutes, and it also settles whether the new LSD ranges are car-specific or fleet-wide.

# 7. ⚠️ Pace — not a clean comparison, and it should not be treated as one

| | |
|---|---|
| Median, 12 laps | 1:52.525 |
| Best lap | **1:50.012** (lap 11, full RPM) |
| Pre-patch session medians | 1:49.1 – 1:50.9 |
| Documented Rev B median, 28 laps | 1:49.180 |

**Do not read a grip loss out of this.** Three reasons, and any one of them is enough:

- **First session on new physics**, with materially changed force feedback.
- **Off-track time on 8 of the 12 laps** (0.93 – 3.70 s), including both full-RPM laps.
- **Run order confounds mode with familiarity.** The short-shifted laps came first, while adapting; the full-RPM laps came last, when dialled in. The apparent 2.6 s/lap short-shift cost is therefore **not** a measurement of short-shifting — the documented figure is ~0.5 s/lap and the best short-shifted lap here (1:51.267) is 1.26 s off the best full-RPM lap.

**Pace on v1.71 remains [UNMEASURED].** It needs a session with the modes interleaved, or simply run after the physics stop feeling new.

# 8. ❌ Not captured

- **Tyre gauge readings — none.** `wear_fl/fr/rl/rr` are null on all 12 laps. **Fifth session running.** The wear question is completely untouched on v1.71.
- **Tyre temperatures** were recorded but are not comparable: end-of-run readings were **FL 73.7 / FR 66.4 / RL 76.8 / RR 74.1 °C** at lap 12, against a Rev B baseline taken at **lap 36** (FL 74.5 / FR 68.3 / RL 85.7 / RR 82.6). The front-to-rear gap looks much narrower — **RL−FL of 3.1 °C against 11.2 °C** — which would be a real finding if the stint lengths matched. They do not. **Suggestive, not evidence.**

# 9. What this changes in the knowledge base

**These are consequences of §1, and §1 is the best-evidenced thing in this file. They are still reasoning, not measurement — they belong in the test queue, not on a setup sheet.**

### A6 — "lengthen 1st/2nd/3rd" is now a much weaker argument

The playbook ranks lengthening the lower gears as **the #1 fix for exit traction in GT7**. That ranking was formed against a car that exceeded the wheelspin threshold on **100 % of full-throttle frames in gears 2 through 6**. It now does so on **6.8 % in 4th, 1.1 % in 5th and 0.6 % in 6th**. **The problem the lever was aimed at has largely gone away above 3rd gear.** It remains live in 1st and 2nd (96.9 % and 97.9 %), which is where the dossier always said it mattered most.

### A5 / LSD acceleration — the case for running it low just got weaker, and the range just got longer

Low accel lock is justified by protecting progressive exit traction against wheelspin. **With slip down ~60 %, the optimum should move up.** And the ceiling moved from 60 to 100. **Both point the same way, and the measured 14 was established on v1.70 against the old torque map.** Re-test upward from 14 — this is now the highest-value single-slider test on the car.

### Gearing — re-gear, but not for the reason expected

Vmax fell 3.9 km/h, so 6th is now slightly long for the circuit. **More interesting: with slip down 60 %, the relationship between engine rpm and road speed changed in every gear**, so every corner-exit rpm target in the archive is off — most in the low gears, by up to 5.5 % in 1st.

### Fuel and strategy — the Monza one-stop needs re-deriving

Rev B's one-stop plan rests on **6.566 L/lap full-RPM** and **−20 % short-shifted**. The −20 % survives (§4). The base number is provisionally **+2.6 %** (§3). At 6.736 L/lap the tank covers **14.8 laps** rather than 15.2, and the plan's fuel and tyre limits were the same number by coincidence. **Re-derive once §3 is settled properly.**

# 10. Standing rules added

**Standing Rule 13 — a post-patch number is not a finding until the identical measurement has been run on pre-patch data.** Not compared against a *documented* figure — re-run the same query over the old sessions. The documented figure may be a single reading, differently derived, or simply wrong. This rule was written because §5 nearly produced a phantom finding within ten minutes of opening the database.

**Standing Rule 14 — prefer the distribution to the datapoint.** The Vmax result (§2) is credible because 20 pre-patch sessions establish a range and the new value falls outside it. The fuel result (§3) is provisional because two laps cannot fall outside anything. **When the archive can supply a distribution, use it; the database holds far more of them than the setup sheets ever wrote down.**
