## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment.** This is authored, not borrowed, and the authorship is real. `CompoundBand` paints a full-height racing-colour ring carrying its own two-letter code; `StrikeRow` draws a literal line through an excluded lap and stops it before the controls so the lap stays re-countable; `SpecLine` refuses the stat-card grid; `Plate` breaks its own border where the label crosses it. Every refusal in DESIGN.md's "What this world refuses" is honoured in the build — no sparkline, no radial, no card grid, no decorative left border.

**But the thesis is the three registers, and the three registers are not in the built product.** `QWidget { color: STENCIL }` (theme.py:160-165) matches every editor subclass and beats `QPalette.Text = CRAYON` (theme.py:281). Pixel-sampled and independently confirmed: the Event name, the track combo, a declared ride height, a Car range bound and a measured lap time all render `#E8E4DC`. Declared and measured are the same ink.

The consequence is that four file docstrings and DESIGN.md's central table are false against the build. `event_screen.py` claims "no measured values on it at all"; it has no *declared* values on it at all. What crayon actually does in the shipped app is fill checkboxes, fill primary buttons, draw the focus border and letter the wordmark — which is an accent colour, the thing every dark admin panel has. The register system survives in exactly two places: the compound band on Practice, and `_EvidenceRow` on Strategy.

There is a deeper version of the same fault: **there is no register for derived.** The palette has measured, declared, provisional and struck. So derived borrows — `ASSUMED → STRUCK` collides with disabled and excluded, and two call sites pass `declared=True` for numbers the app computed, one of them `"Box in N"` on the Race screen, the highest-consequence number the product emits.

**Deterministic scan.** The bundled detector ran clean with exit 0 and zero findings — and that is not a clean bill of health. `SCANNABLE_EXTENSIONS` (detector/node/file-system.mjs:26-30) covers markup and script extensions only; `.py` is absent, and `walkDir` against `pitcrew/ui` returned **0 files** against 13 real entries. PRODUCT.md's claim that the web detector does not apply is confirmed by evidence rather than asserted. No web-detector finding in this report is real, because there are none.

Assessment B substituted measurement in code, which found what the detector could not: 11 colour pairs below 4.5:1, the nav rail at 0 focusable widgets, horizontal clipping below 1600px with scrollbars disabled, an 8px cumulative grid drift across the Car screen's four panels, and ~1.05M px of empty bordered plate on Strategy.

**A false positive worth recording.** B's first geometry pass used `QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen` and reported catastrophic clipping — Event +288/+433px, Engineer +547px, a window minimum exceeding the app's own declared size. All of it was an artifact: offscreen Qt has none of Bahnschrift, Bahnschrift Condensed, Cascadia Mono or Consolas, so every text metric inflates. Native with `WA_DontShowOnScreen` gives a 1170x745 minimum and no clipping at 1600x1000. **For this codebase, offscreen Qt silently manufactures layout failures.**

**Visual overlays.** Not applicable — desktop Qt, no DOM to inject into. Eight native 1600x1000 captures were read as images instead, and confirmed as full-window (0 widgets past x=1600).

## Overall Impression

The craft here is unusually high and the point of view is genuine — this is the rare app where the design document describes a real system rather than a mood. Practice is the proof: the rack paints itself with the stint structure and you understand it without a word of instruction.

And the single biggest opportunity is that **the system the whole thing is built on is one CSS-cascade bug away from existing.** The registers are designed, documented, defended in comments, and absent from the pixels. Fixing the `QWidget` colour rule is a few lines. Everything else in this critique is ordinary product work; that one is the difference between a design system and a comment.

## What's Working

**The rack row as one composed object.** `RackRow` + `StrikeRow` + `CompoundBand` collapses three decisions — is this lap evidence, what was it running, what did the gauge say — onto one 54px line, so marking up a stint is a single pass down a column rather than eleven modal detours. The detail that earns it: the strike stops before the controls, because a line through a combo box reads as *disabled* and an excluded lap still needs its compound.

**Principles enforced in the widget layer, not the style guide.** `_WheelGuard` stops a mouse wheel silently rewriting a setup value and forwards the event so the page still scrolls. The `EMPTY` sentinel renders "—", exports `null`, and is filtered out of `values()`. Most products with a "missing is never zero" principle have it in prose; this one has it in the widgets, which is why the Event screenshot shows real dashes in the damper fields rather than a wall of zeros.

**`_EvidenceRow` on Strategy.** The only widget that dual-codes provenance: the value takes the ink of its source *and* carries the word beneath it — measured, entered, assumed, not known. It works because colour alone fails the moment two inks collapse, which is exactly what has happened everywhere else. It is also the template for the repair.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Crayon never reaches a value — declared and measured render in the same ink**
- **Why it matters:** This is the product's first principle and its stated design thesis, silently absent. A range the driver *declared* is pixel-identical to a lap time that came *off the stream*. It also makes DESIGN.md and four docstrings false against the build, so the next person to work here will trust a claim the pixels refute.
- **Fix:** Remove `color` from the `QWidget` rule and set it on `QLabel`, or set `color: CRAYON` explicitly on the editor rule while keeping `PlaceholderText` on the palette. Then add a rendering test that grabs one editor and one `Measured` label and asserts their inks differ — this failure is invisible to every test that does not look at pixels, and all 610 currently pass.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

**[P0] No register for derived, so derived borrows one and collides**
- **Why it matters:** CLAUDE.md §4.5 and PRODUCT.md principle 3 both forbid presenting derived as measured or declared. The app has no ink with which to obey. `ASSUMED → STRUCK` puts modelled values in the disabled/excluded ink; `race_screen.py:214` paints `"Box in N"` — a modelled call acted on at racing speed — in the ink that means the driver typed it.
- **Fix:** Add a fourth ink for derived, distinct from `STRUCK`; correct those two call sites; adopt `_EvidenceRow`'s dual coding wherever a derived number appears alone.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize`

**[P1] `STRUCK` at 2.93:1 is the app's instructional-text colour**
- **Why it matters:** Two failures at once. Semantically, `STRUCK` means *removed from the count* — so instructions are painted in the ink for things that do not count. Physically, `#6B6459` on `SHOULDER` measures **2.93:1**, below even the 3:1 large-text floor, and it carries every field hint, every unit suffix, the Practice column headers at 10px, and the Engineer instruction for the field its own code calls "the most valuable field on the screen." 28 text sites. This is read from the driving position with a headset just pushed up.
- **Fix:** Promote instructional prose and column headers to `STENCIL_DIM` (5.70:1); confine `STRUCK` to placeholders, disabled controls and struck rows — which is what DESIGN.md's table already says it is for. Also fix `band_ink()`, which uses NTSC luminance rather than WCAG and picks illegible ink on 6 of 11 compounds (IM 2.53:1, SS 2.84:1).
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

**[P1] The entire primary navigation is keyboard-unreachable, and there are no shortcuts anywhere**
- **Why it matters:** The nav rail is eight `StencilLabel`s with `mousePressEvent` monkey-patched on (app.py:82) — 0 focusable, all `NoFocus`. No `QShortcut`, no `setShortcut`, no `setTabOrder` in the codebase. The sole user does this every week and the app makes him work at beginner speed forever. Worth stating precisely: *within* the screens all 355 controls are focusable, so the gap is the rail plus missing focus-visible styling on checkboxes.
- **Fix:** Make the rail items focusable with a visible focus ring and give them `Ctrl+1…8`; add shortcuts for Save, Export and Generate; add `QCheckBox::indicator:focus` to the stylesheet, matching the button rule that already does this correctly.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Strategy and Race rest as large empty plates with no guidance**
- **Why it matters:** Measured from the captures: Strategy shows ~1.05M px of empty bordered plate; Race's "EVERY CALL" is ~1360x740px of nothing with "ON THE RADIO" holding a single dash. Neither names its prerequisite. Race is the screen looked at *after* the headset comes off, and a blank call log cannot be told from "nothing was recorded" or "it crashed again" — which matters more now that the app finally leaves a crash trace.
- **Fix:** Give both an empty state inside the plate that names what is missing and what produces it — Strategy: an event, counted laps, a tagged compound, a wear reading; Race: an approved plan. State why the disabled primary is disabled.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable onboard`

**Also confirmed, below the top five:** the per-lap toggle reads COUNT on laps that are already counted and renders an empty bordered button on structurally-excluded rows; plate titles are 9px cap-height while the field captions inside them are 11px, so the only chunking device is the least legible text on a 23-key form; horizontal clipping appears below 1600px wide (Event +96px, Engineer +107px at 1280x800) with `ScrollBarAlwaysOff`, making those pixels unreachable; the Car screen's MIN/MAX columns drift ~8px across four panels; `StrikeRow.clicked` is declared, painted with a hover highlight, and never emitted or connected.

## Persona Red Flags

**The expert power user (Leon, weekly, knows the domain cold).** No keyboard path anywhere — not to the eight screens, not to Save, Export or Generate; the nav labels are not even focusable. No bulk tagging: an 11-lap stint on one compound is eleven separate combo boxes. No filter across 608 cars. He is the only user this app will ever have, and it is built for a first-timer who never arrives.

**The same person three weeks later, after a gap.** Nothing says where he left off. The rail shows eight equal peers with no completion state, though the store knows there are 11 laps, no approved plan and an unverified range record. Change the car on the Car screen and it silently diverges from the Event's car, with no warning on either. The copy will send him to "the tune builder" and "the Driver Feedback tab" — a tool this app retired.

**Reading in low light with the headset just off — his actual physical state.** Instructional copy at 2.93:1 at 10–13px; the Practice column headers effectively invisible; disabled, placeholder, hint and struck all rendered in shades that are hard to separate. And with crayon and stencil currently collapsed into one ink, the provenance system — which outside `_EvidenceRow` is colour-only — carries no information at all.

## Minor Observations

- `MarkButton` uppercases only at construction, so `set_listening()` hand-uppercases "STOP LISTENING". Move the transform into `setText`.
- Mixed dash convention in user-visible copy: ASCII `" - "` on Event and Practice against proper em dashes on Car, Engineer and Reference.
- The 11 compound chips on Event are one flat row, though `theme.COMPOUND_BANDS` already knows the Racing/Sports/Comfort families that would split them into three groups of ≤5.
- Practice offers per-lap WEAR F / WEAR R spin boxes; in the one real 11-lap session captured, all 22 read "—".
- Two screens have no footer note. Reference is defensible by design; Race is not.
- Page bottom margin splits 5/3 across screens (`0` vs `24`) with no structural distinction.
- Exactly one hard-coded colour literal outside `theme.py` (`widgets.py:295`) — near-total discipline. Emoji/glyph ban fully respected: 0 chars in category `So`.
- The window floors at 1170x745, set by Practice's fixed lap columns (width) and Settings (height) — two screens dictate the minimum size for all eight.

## Questions to Consider

1. **If crayon never reaches a value, was the register system ever a design system — or has it only ever been a comment?** Four docstrings and DESIGN.md's central table assert it; the pixels refute it; all 610 tests pass. What would have to exist in this build for that gap to be impossible?
2. **Practice asks for a tyre-gauge reading per lap — for a gauge he cannot see while driving and will not recall afterwards — and in the one real session captured, all 22 boxes are empty.** Is per-lap the wrong grain for the only human corroboration of the wear model?
3. **The stated positioning is that the app observes and never authors — yet the one handoff it declines to automate is parsing the reply it just filed into the form it already owns.** Is that protecting the principle, or is it the parser nobody wrote?
4. **Strategy and Race serve job 4; Event, Car, Practice and Engineer serve jobs 1–3.** The rail presents all six as peers of each other and of Reference and Settings. Are these one app with eight screens, or two modes sharing a store — and would naming that split make the rail solve itself?
