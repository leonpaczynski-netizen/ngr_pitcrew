Rear stability stack — the alternative to front brake bias

    
GT7 has no engine-braking map, so LSD braking sensitivity is the off-throttle rear toolkit.

    

      
PriorityLeverDirectionNote

      
        
1LSD braking sensitivityHigher, +2 at a timeStop when turn-in starts to dull

The GT7 traps

    

      
Camber ≠ turn-inIn GT7 camber acts mid-corner and costs braking and traction immediately. Start 1.0–1.5°, not 3.0.

      
Turn-in is owned byFront compression damping (lower), front ARB, front toe, brake balance. Not camber.

      
Front toe is contestedMay be inverted vs real life. A/B −0.05 / 0.00 / +0.05 per car. Never run toe-in at the front.

      
Wheelspin is a gearbox problemLengthening 1st/2nd/3rd is the top-ranked GT7 traction fix, ahead of every diff and spring change.

      
Max Speed slider wipes ratiosIt's a generator, not a trim. Set it first, then final drive, then individual gears. Never touch it again.

      
Kerbs = ride heightPost-1.49, raise ride height 3–5 mm first. A car that suddenly won't turn is arch rub, not balance.

      
Brake balance signnegative = FRONT, positive = REAR. It's a delta from each car's own factory bias.

      
Chassis tuning is freeSuspension, gears and LSD values cost zero PP. Fitting the parts costs PP. Aero is not free.

      
Aero ≠ lap time~1,300 lb of downforce measured at ~0.1 s/lap. Use aero for balance, not for grip.

      
Discard any guide mentioningTyre pressure, caster, brake pressure, high/low-speed damper split. None exist in GT7.
